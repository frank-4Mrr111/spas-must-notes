Formula Sheet - SMS 3263

Annuity-immediate: a-angle-n = (1 - v^n)/i | Accumulated: s-angle-n = ((1+i)^n - 1)/i | Increasing annuity: (Ia)-angle-n = (a-double-dot-angle-n - n v^n)/i | Decreasing: (Da)-angle-n = (n - a-angle-n)/i | Loan: Payment = Loan / a-angle-n | Bond: Price = Coupon x a-angle-n + Redemption x v^n | Duration: Weighted average time | NPV: PV inflows - Cost | IRR: Rate where NPV = 0

Glossary - SMS 3263

Increasing annuity: payments rise by 1, 2, 3... Decreasing annuity: payments fall. Sinking fund: saving to repay loan at end. Capital redemption: saving to replace asset. Duration: average time to receive money. Immunisation: protecting against interest change. Matching: assets match liabilities. NPV: net present value. IRR: internal rate of return. Payback: time to recover cost.

Topic 1: Revision - Annuities and Discounting

Estimated time: ~30 minutes | Objectives: Revise PV, AV, annuity-immediate and annuity-due, choose correct formula.

Quick revision: PV moves money to today, AV moves money to future. Annuity-immediate pays at end, annuity-due pays at start.

PV of annuity-immediate: Payment x a-angle-n, a-angle-n = (1 - v^n)/i.

Example: 10000 at end of each year for 5 years, i=10%. v=0.9091, v^5=0.6209, a-angle-5 = (1-0.6209)/0.10 = 3.7908. PV = 37908.

AV: Payment x s-angle-n, s-angle-n = ((1+i)^n - 1)/i.

Topic 2: Increasing and Decreasing Annuities

Estimated time: ~40 minutes | Objectives: Explain increasing / decreasing annuities, calculate PV, explain when they are used.

Not all annuities are level. Pensions often rise with inflation. Loans sometimes fall.

Increasing annuity-immediate (Ia): Payments 1, 2, 3, ..., n. PV = (Ia)-angle-n = (a-double-dot-angle-n - n v^n)/i.

Decreasing annuity (Da): Payments n, n-1, ..., 1. PV = (Da)-angle-n = (n - a-angle-n)/i.

Example: Increasing annuity, n=3, i=10%. a-double-dot-3 = 2.7355, v^3=0.7513. (Ia) = (2.7355 - 3 x 0.7513)/0.10 = (2.7355 - 2.2539)/0.10 = 4.816. If first payment is 1000, PV = 4816.

Topic 3: Loans, Sinking Funds and Capital Redemption

Estimated time: ~35 minutes | Objectives: Explain loan, sinking fund, capital redemption, calculate instalment and sinking fund payment.

There are two ways to repay a loan: 1. Amortisation - pay interest + capital each time. 2. Sinking fund - pay interest each time, save capital in a fund to pay at end.

Amortisation: Payment = Loan / a-angle-n.

Sinking fund: Interest payment = Loan x i each period. Sinking fund payment = Loan / s-angle-n, where s-angle-n is accumulated annuity.

Example: Loan 100000, i=10%, n=3. Amortisation payment = 100000 / 2.4869 = 40211. Sinking fund: Interest = 10000 per year. s-angle-3 = 3.31. Sinking payment = 100000 / 3.31 = 30211. Total per year = 40211. Same total! Different split.

Topic 4: Bonds, Yields and Duration

Estimated time: ~35 minutes | Objectives: Explain bond price, calculate price, explain yield, explain duration.

Bond price = PV of coupons + PV of redemption. If interest rises, bond price falls. Duration measures how sensitive price is to interest change.

Example: Bond, coupon 8000 per year for 3 years, redemption 100000, yield 10%. a-angle-3 = 2.4869, v^3 = 0.7513. Price = 8000 x 2.4869 + 100000 x 0.7513 = 19895 + 75130 = 95025.

Duration: Weighted average time to receive money. Longer duration = more sensitive to interest change.

Topic 5: Immunisation and Matching

Estimated time: ~30 minutes | Objectives: Explain immunisation, explain matching, explain conditions for immunisation.

Immunisation protects an insurer when interest rates change. If assets and liabilities move together, the insurer stays safe.

Matching: Assets = Liabilities in amount and timing. Example: Need to pay 100000 in 5 years, hold a bond that pays 100000 in 5 years.

Immunisation conditions (Redington): 1. PV Assets = PV Liabilities. 2. Duration Assets = Duration Liabilities. 3. Convexity Assets > Convexity Liabilities.

Why it matters: If rates rise, assets fall but liabilities fall more, so you still can pay.

Topic 6: Project Appraisal - NPV, IRR and Payback

Estimated time: ~35 minutes | Objectives: Explain NPV, IRR, payback, calculate and decide.

Project appraisal decides: Should we invest? Use NPV, IRR, Payback together, not just one.

NPV: PV inflows - Cost. NPV > 0 accept.

IRR: Rate where NPV = 0. IRR > cost of capital accept.

Payback: Time to recover cost. Shorter is safer, but ignores money after payback and time value.

Example: Cost 100000, inflow 60000 in year 1, 60000 in year 2, i=10%. PV = 60000 x 0.9091 + 60000 x 0.8264 = 54546 + 49584 = 104130. NPV = 4130. Accept.
